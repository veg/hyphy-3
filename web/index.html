How WALRUS Works • Metric Glossary & FAQ

Stage 0 — Homology Admission & 6-Frame Curation Input: Raw FASTA / NEXUS
Translates every sequence across all 6 reading frames (+1, +2, +3, −1, −2, −3) under the active genetic code and scores 3-mer amino-acid homology against the cohort core. Automatically rescues shifted frames or reverse-complemented cDNAs, trims 5′/3′ non-coding UTR flanks at the nearest Met/stop boundary, recommends alternative genetic codes (e.g., Vertebrate Mitochondrial) if internal stops vanish, and quarantines non-homologous paralogs, short fragments, or stop-ridden pseudogenes before they corrupt downstream alignment and tree inference.
Gate: Homology Z ≥ 3.5 • Sense Codons ≥ 15 • Internal Stops = 0 (Manual checkboxes override any sequence)
Stage 1 — Codon Pair-HMM + POA Alignment & Frameshift QC Input: Admitted CDS
Constructs a reading-frame-preserving codon alignment via progressive Partial Order Alignment (POA) coupled with a Codon Pair-HMM. Computes exact Forward–Backward marginal posterior probabilities (P_hom) for every aligned codon column, detects and repairs 1-bp / 2-bp frameshift indels via dynamic programming against the cohort consensus, enumerates competing gap-placement paths in low-confidence windows, and polishes indel boundaries under the MG94 codon likelihood.
Column Confidence: P_hom ≥ 95% (High) • 80–95% (Moderate) • < 80% (Ambiguous POA Window)
Stage 2 — Phylogeny, Rooting & Recombination Routing Input: Codon Alignment
Estimates or refines the phylogeny via coupled Subtree Pruning & Regrafting (SPR) under the MG94×REV codon model, audits 3rd-codon-position GC compositional drift (GC3%), and scans for intragenic recombination breakpoints using a sliding-window ΔBIC tournament. Confirmed mosaic segments are routed to local SPR topologies so recombination artifacts cannot masquerade as positive selection. Clicking any node lets you tag custom {Foreground} clades for Stage 3.
Recombination Routing: ΔBIC > 0 & Discordant Window Tree → Partitioned Segment Topology
Stage 3 — Unified Selection & Evolutionary Forensics Input: Alignment + Tagged Tree
Executes a joint suite of codon models in a single pass: BUSTED (gene-wide episodic selection), FEL (pervasive site α/β), MEME (episodic site β+), Contrast-FEL (clade-differential sites), RELAX (clade selection intensity K), aBSREL (lineage adaptive bursts & purifying-core LoF erosion), PRIME (physicochemical property exchangeability), and Phylogenetic Epistasis (co-substitution on shared branches), complete with Empirical Bayes ancestral codon-substitution trees for every site.
Click any lollipop, arc, or table row in Stage 3 to inspect the exact ancestral codon-substitution tree
BUSTED (ω₃ & Gene-Wide p) Stage 3 • Gene-Wide
Branch-Site Unrestricted Statistical Test for Episodic Diversification. Tests whether at least one site on at least one branch experienced positive selection (ω₃ > 1) relative to a constrained null model where ω₃ ≤ 1.
ω₁ ≤ ω₂ ≤ 1 ≤ ω₃ (Weight p₃%) • LRT p ≤ 0.05
FEL (α, β, p, BH q) Stage 3 • Site Pervasive
Fixed Effects Likelihood. Estimates site-specific synonymous rate (α) and tree-wide nonsynonymous rate (β) by maximum likelihood. Detects pervasive diversifying (β > α) or pervasive purifying (β < α) selection.
H₀: β = α vs. H₁: β ≠ α (1 d.o.f. χ² LRT)
MEME (β+, Weight p+, p, BH q) Stage 3 • Site Episodic
Mixed Effects Model of Evolution. Allows each codon site to evolve under purifying/neutral selection (β⁻ ≤ α) on most branches while bursting into positive selection (β+ > α) on a subset proportion (p+) of lineages.
Detects episodic bursts masked by tree-wide purifying constraint
RELAX (K) & Contrast-FEL Stage 3 • Clade Regime
RELAX tests whether selection stringency on a focal clade is Intensified (K > 1) or Relaxed (K < 1) via ω_test = ω_ref^K. Contrast-FEL identifies individual codons with significantly different β rates between clades.
K > 1: Intensification • K < 1: Relaxation toward neutrality
aBSREL & LoF Core Erosion Stage 3 • Branch Level
aBSREL tests each branch for episodic positive selection (ω+ > 1). LoF Erosion computes a Bayes Factor testing whether a lineage accumulates radical amino-acid changes specifically at sites that are strictly conserved (purifying core) across the rest of the tree.
Distinguishes adaptive bursts from pseudogenization / relaxed purifying core
PRIME (Physicochemical Exchangeability) Stage 3 • AA Properties
Tests whether nonsynonymous substitutions at a site conserve or radically toggle five amino-acid properties: Hydrophobicity, Polarity, Isoelectric Point (pI / Charge), Volume, and Secondary-Structure Propensity.
Identifies property-driven toggles (e.g., Charge Switch, Hydrophobic Core)
Phylogenetic Epistasis (Cosine r, p, q) Stage 3 • Co-Evolution
Detects pairs of codons that co-substitute on the same phylogenetic branches more often than expected by chance. Orthogonalizes site branch vectors against genome-wide branch mutation load before testing cosine similarity r and the exact finite-branch hypergeometric tail.
Click any Manhattan arc to compare both codons' substitution trees side by side
Simes Omnibus p, BH FDR q & AA N_eff Statistical Calibration
Simes p combines site-level p-values across FEL/MEME/dFEL into a single omnibus test per codon. BH q controls the Benjamini–Hochberg False Discovery Rate across the gene. AA N_eff = exp(H) measures the effective number of amino acids tolerated at a site (1.0 = invariant, 20.0 = uniform).
Headline significance gate: p ≤ 0.05 and BH FDR q ≤ 0.10
Do my sequences or trees ever leave my computer? Privacy & Security
No. Every stage of WALRUS runs 100% locally inside your browser tab via compiled C++20 SIMD128 WebAssembly (hyphy_wasm.js inside a Web Worker). No sequence data, tree topologies, or results are ever uploaded to an external server.
When should I use "Step-by-Step Workbench" vs. "⚡ Full Auto Pipeline"? Workflow
Use Step-by-Step when working with raw/uncurated sequences where you want to inspect quarantined sequences in Stage 0, review frameshift repairs or POA uncertainty in Stage 1, or manually click a foreground clade on the Stage 2 tree before running Stage 3. Use ⚡ Full Auto when you want WALRUS to chain Stages 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 automatically with default curation rules.
How do I test a specific foreground clade for RELAX and Contrast-FEL? Stage 2 → Stage 3
You have two ways: (1) Include standard HyPhy branch tags in your Newick tree (e.g., (Human{PR},Chimp{PR})Node1{PR}), which WALRUS automatically detects, or (2) In Stage 2 (Phylogeny), click any internal node or leaf on the interactive tree and click Toggle Whole Clade {Foreground} in the slide-over drawer before advancing to Stage 3.
What if my input file is already aligned or already has a tree? Input Handling
If your input FASTA/NEXUS is already codon-aligned, Stage 0 preserves your existing gap structure by default (or lets you toggle Strip Gaps & De Novo Realign under ⚙ Thresholds & Code). If your NEXUS/FASTA contains an embedded Newick tree, Stage 2 uses your topology as the starting tree and optimizes codon branch lengths and SPR moves on top of it.
Why was a sequence quarantined in Stage 0, and can I force-admit it? Stage 0 Curation
Sequences are quarantined if all 6 reading frames contain internal stop codons, fall below the minimum sense-codon length, or have a 3-mer homology Z-score below threshold (indicating a non-homologous contaminant or paralog). Click any sequence row in Stage 0 to inspect its 6-frame stop-codon barcode and nearest-neighbor alignment, or check its row checkbox to force-admit it.
How do I interpret columns with Homology Confidence P_hom < 95% in Stage 1? Stage 1 Alignment
In indel-rich regions, multiple gap placements can yield similar alignment scores. Stage 1 computes the Forward–Backward posterior probability P_hom that each aligned codon column represents true positional homology. Columns with P_hom < 95% are highlighted in amber/orange on the minimap, and clicking the corresponding Indel Window event displays the competing MAP vs. alternative gap placements and their posterior weights.
